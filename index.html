Privacy Policy

RESINCOT SACCO Mobile App · Last updated: August 11, 2026

Resincot Sacco Society Ltd ("Resincot Sacco," "we," "us," or "our") operates the RESINCOT SACCO mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect from members who use the App, how we use it, and the choices you have.

By creating an account or using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this policy.

Note: some contact details and organizational information below are placeholders pending confirmation from Resincot Sacco's management. This page should be updated once those are finalized.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information you provide directly

  • Account and registration details: full name, email address, phone number, national ID number, membership/account number, and password.
  • Loan application data: loan type, amount, period, purpose, and supporting information you submit when applying for a loan.
  • Uploaded documents: identification documents, payslips, and e-signature images you choose to upload when applying for a loan or completing verification. These are selected from your device's photo library or file storage using your explicit permission.
  • Guarantor information: where you add or are added as a guarantor on a loan, related account and identification details are processed to complete that request.
  • Support and communication data: any information you provide when contacting us for support, e.g. via email.

1.2 Information collected automatically

  • Account activity: login times, session activity, and actions taken within the App (e.g. generating a statement), used to keep your session secure and to detect inactivity.
  • Technical data: basic device and connection information needed for the App to function (e.g. network connectivity status). The App requests only internet and network-state permissions; it does not access your location, contacts, or camera in the background.

1.3 Information from our core banking system

When you log in, the App retrieves your account balances, savings, share capital, loan accounts, and transaction statements from Resincot Sacco's core banking system in order to display them to you.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information described above to:

  • Create and authenticate your member account (login, OTP verification, password reset).
  • Display your account balances, savings, loan status, and transaction statements.
  • Process loan applications, including document and guarantor verification.
  • Generate and deliver account/loan statements (including PDF statements you download or email to yourself).
  • Provide customer support and respond to your enquiries.
  • Maintain the security of your account and detect fraudulent or unauthorized activity.
  • Comply with legal, regulatory, and SASRA reporting obligations applicable to savings and credit co-operatives in Kenya.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

3. How We Share Information

We share information only where necessary to operate the App and the Sacco's services:

  • Core banking / IT service providers who host and operate the backend systems the App connects to, strictly to process your requests (e.g. checking balances, submitting a loan application).
  • Payment partners (e.g. M-PESA, partner banks) when you make a deposit, repayment, or contribution through those channels.
  • Regulators and legal authorities, where required by Kenyan law, including the Sacco Societies Regulatory Authority (SASRA) and other applicable bodies.
  • Guarantors and co-borrowers, limited to the information needed to process a loan or guarantee request you or another member initiated.

We do not share your information with advertisers or unrelated third parties for marketing purposes.

4. Data Storage and Security

  • Your account credentials and session data are stored securely on your device using standard mobile storage mechanisms and are cleared when you log out or clear the app cache.
  • Communication between the App and our servers is encrypted in transit.
  • Access to member data on our backend systems is restricted to authorized personnel.
  • While we take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. Data Retention

We retain your personal and account information for as long as your membership with Resincot Sacco is active, and afterward only as long as necessary to comply with legal, regulatory, tax, or accounting requirements (including SASRA record-keeping requirements).

6. Your Rights

Under the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Request deletion of your data, subject to our legal and regulatory retention obligations (see Section 7 below).
  • Object to or restrict certain processing of your data.
  • Lodge a complaint with the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC), Kenya.

To exercise these rights, contact us using the details in Section 9.

7. Data Deletion Requests

You may request deletion of your account data by contacting us at info@resincotsacco.co.ke (placeholder — confirm real support email) or through the App under Settings → Legal → Data Deletion Request.

We will delete or anonymize all data not subject to such requirements.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for use by Resincot Sacco members who are 18 years of age or older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
